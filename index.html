About the Partnership

Rutgers' Center for Advanced Infrastructure and Transportation (CAIT) partners with transit agencies to bring together university research and operational expertise, developing practical, scalable solutions for real-world transportation challenges.

  • Data analytics
  • Optimization
  • Machine learning
  • Decision support
  • Interactive visualization
